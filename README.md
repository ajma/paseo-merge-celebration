# Paseo merge celebration 🎉

**You shipped it. Enjoy the moment.**

A little celebration for a lot of hard work. When your pull request is merged and its workspace is archived, colorful confetti falls across Paseo, with a recap of what you just shipped.

![Colorful confetti falling over a sample Paseo workspace, with a “Work shipped!” recap and a Dismiss button](docs/celebration-preview.png)

_Staged screenshot: the real celebration UI over a fictional workspace._

## A proper send-off for finished work

- **Celebrate automatically.** Merge your PR, archive the workspace, and let the confetti fly.
- **Remember the win.** See the PR title and a short recap of linked issues.
- **Keep your flow.** Your current screen stays right where it is, ready to use.
- **Enjoy it as long as you like.** Hit **Dismiss** when you’re done, and the last pieces drift offscreen.
- **Keep it calm.** Reduced motion gives you the recap without the animation.

Unmerged work stays quiet. Each archive gets its moment, without old celebrations popping up when you reconnect.

## Try a little joy

Open the Command Center with **⌘K** (or **Ctrl+K**) and choose **Preview merge celebration**. No merge needed. No workspace archived.

Made for **Paseo 0.11.2 on desktop and in the browser**, with GitHub pull requests.
